Biweekly lawn maintenance in Fort Lauderdale & Tamarac

Two visits a month, on a set schedule. Your lawn is mowed, edged and cleaned up every other week, so it always looks finished and you never have to think about it.

Every visit includes

  • Mowing at the right height for your grass
  • Edging along sidewalks, driveways and curbs
  • String trimming around trees, fences and beds
  • Clippings and debris blown off hard surfaces
  • A quick look at the lawn's health

Who it's for

Homeowners who want the yard ready for guests any day of the month, and small businesses that need a clean, professional front lawn.

Starting with an overgrown yard? We'll begin with a yard cleanup, then keep it in shape with the plan.

Plan questions

How does the biweekly schedule work?

We visit every other week on the same day of the week, so you get two visits a month.

Can I add other services?

Yes. Hedge and shrub trimming, weed control and fertilization, and yard cleanups can be added to the plan.

Can I change my visit day?

Call or message us and we'll find a day that works.

What if it rains on my visit day?

If weather stops a visit, we'll let you know and move it to the next available day.

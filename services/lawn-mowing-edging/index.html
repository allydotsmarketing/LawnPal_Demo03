Cut for South Florida grass

Most lawns here are St. Augustine grass, which stays healthiest when it's kept on the taller side. In the rainy season, from late spring through fall, grass grows fast, and that's when regular visits matter most.

Add it to the biweekly plan

Want the yard handled without booking every visit? The biweekly maintenance plan covers mowing and edging twice a month, and lawn mowing & edging can be part of it.

Questions about lawn mowing & edging

How often should my lawn be mowed?

In the rainy season most South Florida lawns need a cut every week or every two weeks. In the drier months, every two weeks is usually enough.

Do you clean up after mowing?

Yes. Every visit ends with clippings blown off sidewalks, driveways and patios.

Do you mow commercial properties?

Yes. We take care of homes and small commercial properties.
